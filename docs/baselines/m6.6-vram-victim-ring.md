# #139 m6.6: the VRAM victim ring takes exclusive-residency D2H off the refill path

Machine: 32 threads, 93 GiB RAM, 24 GiB VRAM (gfx1100). Model: GLM-5.3-Flash UD-Q4_K_XL, 6 split shards, one replica (`/home`, 9100 PRO): the manifest was copied with its unmounted `/mnt/data` replica removed. Measured 2026-11-02 on c740103 + this change (uncommitted).

Cold = shards evicted with `scripts/evict-model-cache.py` right before the leg, which then ran as `ab-bench.sh <leg> warm` (its `drop_caches` needs a password here), so `meta.txt` says `temp=warm` for every leg. Expert reads are `O_DIRECT`, so cold differs from warm only in dense-weight page cache.

## Decode (`scripts/ab-bench.sh`, 63 GiB pool, 128 tokens, ctx 1024, empty config dir)

Order inc → exc → ring, then ring → exc → inc. Greedy output was byte-identical in every leg. Reproduce the table with `scripts/ab-report.py <work-dir> <legs>`.

| leg | tok/s cold | tok/s warm | disk GB/token | device cache K |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| inclusive | 4.247, 4.236 | 4.278, 4.270 | 0.88 | 17 |
| exclusive | 3.211, 3.184 | 3.205, 3.212 | 0.85 | 17 |
| exclusive + `--victim-ring 16` | 3.901, 3.912 | 3.901, 3.896 | 0.87 | 16 |

Means: inclusive 4.258, exclusive 3.203, ring 3.903 tok/s. The ring recovers ≈ 0.66 of the 1.055 tok/s exclusive loses (−24.8% → −8.3% against inclusive), despite giving up one cache slot per layer for its VRAM. It never filled (`victim_ring_full=0`; whole run: 94 359 units over 31 453 evictions). In decode rows its D2H moved 2.97 GB/token in 118.9 ms/token of D2H-stream wall time, against a ≈ 256 ms token. The disk saving at 63 GiB is ≈ 0.01–0.03 GB/token, too small to pay for the remaining gap, so inclusive stays the default.

Ring VRAM: 16 × 3 × 6.88 MB (largest `bytes_per_expert`, 6 881 280 B) ≈ 330 MB ≈ 1.3% of 24 GiB.
