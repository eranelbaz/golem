## Design arithmetic (target: GLM-5.3-Flash UD-Q4_K_XL, 24 GiB VRAM, 93 GiB RAM)

- Routed set per step at 4 slots: 288 × (1 − (1 − 8/288)^4) ≈ 30.7 unique
  experts/layer vs 8 solo → up to ≈ 3.8× bytes moved per step on misses;
  aggregate tok/s is expected ≈ flat (D-13), each slot ≈ ¼. Default stays 1.
- Per-slot memory: recurrent state 145.56 MiB × 4 = 582 MiB VRAM; KV
  19,712 B × n_ctx × 4 (Task 7 plan terms). Context: `n_ctx = config.n_ctx ×
  parallel`, `n_seq_max = parallel`, `kv_unified = false` so every slot gets
  `n_ctx_seq ≥ config.n_ctx` (tested at parallel 4).
- Short joiner chunk T below the ≈ 85 % routed-share switch: 1 − (1 − 8/288)^T
  (T = 32 → 59 %) → ≈ 2.6 GB/layer vs 4.4 GB whole; streamed chunks run alone.
- Stream fairness interval `STREAM_EVERY = 32` decode steps (`slot.rs`),
  from #49's measured cost: one 512-token streamed chunk ≈ 91 s cold
  (`m3.6-prefill-stream.md`, on512 mean 104 s incl. ≈ 10 s load).

## Determinism at batch > 1 (tiny fixtures, HIP)

`batch_tiny_moe.rs` asserts 4 concurrent greedy slots produce **byte-identical**
text to their solo runs on both tiny-moe and tiny-hybrid (per-seq recurrent
state). No reduction-order delta observed at these shapes; the T2 tolerance
fallback is therefore not used. On the target, batch > 1 changes the
`MUL_MAT_ID` ubatch shape (cf. the kernel-selection delta noted in
`m3.6-prefill-stream.md`), so a delta there is possible and must be recorded
here by the HW leg.
