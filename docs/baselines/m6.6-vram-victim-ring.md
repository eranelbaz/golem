# #139 m6.6: the VRAM victim ring takes exclusive-residency D2H off the refill path

Machine: 32 threads, 93 GiB RAM, 24 GiB VRAM (gfx1100). Model: GLM-5.3-Flash UD-Q4_K_XL, 6 split shards, one replica (`/home`, 9100 PRO): the manifest was copied with its unmounted `/mnt/data` replica removed. Remeasured for #204 on committed b2c90c7 (release build, `git=` in every `meta.txt`).

Cold = `ab-bench.sh <leg> cold`: `sync` + `drop_caches` right before the leg, so `meta.txt` says `temp=cold`. Warm = the same leg rerun right after its cold run. Expert reads are `O_DIRECT` and every process starts with an empty expert pool, so cold differs from warm only in dense-weight page cache.

## Decode (`scripts/ab-bench.sh`, 63 GiB pool, 128 tokens, ctx 1024, empty config dir)

Order inc → exc → ring, then ring → exc → inc (cold then warm per leg). Greedy output was byte-identical in all 12 legs. Reproduce the table with `scripts/ab-report.py <work-dir> <legs>`.

| leg | tok/s cold | tok/s warm | disk GB/token | device cache K |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| inclusive | 4.232, 4.185 | 4.239, 4.228 | 0.88 | 17 |
| exclusive | 3.197, 3.181 | 3.192, 3.200 | 0.85 | 17 |
| exclusive + `--victim-ring 16` | 3.823, 3.846 | 3.864, 3.884 | 0.87 | 16 |

Means (cold / warm): inclusive 4.209 / 4.234, exclusive 3.189 / 3.196, ring 3.835 / 3.874 tok/s. Over all four runs, the ring recovers ≈ 0.66 of the 1.03 tok/s that exclusive loses (−24.4% → −8.7% against inclusive), even though it gives up one cache slot per layer for its VRAM. Cold costs the ring ≈ 1% and the other legs ≈ 0.5% or less. It never filled (`victim_ring_full=0`; whole run: 94 359 units over 31 453 evictions). In decode rows its D2H moved 2.97 GB/token in 119–121 ms/token of D2H-stream wall time, against a ≈ 260 ms token. The disk saving at 63 GiB is ≈ 0.01–0.03 GB/token, too small to pay for the remaining gap, so inclusive stays the default.

Ring VRAM: 16 × 3 × 6.88 MB (largest `bytes_per_expert`, 6 881 280 B) ≈ 330 MB ≈ 1.3% of 24 GiB.
